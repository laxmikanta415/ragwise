# Refund Policy

## Overview

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. No questions asked.

## Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:
- Your request is within 30 days of the original purchase date
- You have not exceeded 10,000 API calls on the paid plan
- Your account is in good standing (no violations of our Terms of Service)

## How to Request a Refund

1. Email support@company.com with subject line: `Refund Request - [your account email]`
2. Include your order ID (found in your billing dashboard)
3. Our team will process the refund within 3–5 business days
4. Refunds are returned to the original payment method

## Partial Refunds

For annual plan subscribers who cancel mid-year, we calculate a prorated refund based on unused months remaining.

**Formula:** Refund = (Monthly rate × Months remaining) × 0.85

The 15% processing fee covers payment gateway costs.

## Non-Refundable Items

- Add-on usage fees (overage charges beyond your plan limit)
- Custom enterprise setup fees
- One-time onboarding sessions

## Contact

Questions about refunds? Contact billing@company.com or use the in-app chat.
