## FAQ

**Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?**
Yes. Changes take effect immediately. Prorated billing applies.

**Is there a free trial for Pro?**
Yes — 14-day free trial, no credit card required.

**What happens if I exceed my document limit?**
You'll receive an email warning at 80% usage. Ingestion pauses at 100% until you upgrade or delete documents.

**Do you offer discounts for annual billing?**
Yes — 20% discount on Pro and Business plans with annual billing.
